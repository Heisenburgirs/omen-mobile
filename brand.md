# OMEN mobile: look

Dark, flat, in the manner of Cash App. The ground is a deep navy, not black;
surfaces step up from it; nothing is outlined or shaded. Green and red mean
money moving; white marks what is selected or a link. No blue accents on
icons or text.

Tokens live in `src/theme.ts` (`tradingColors`): ground `#070B19`, surfaces
`#141720` / `#1B1F2A`, secondary text `#9A9DA8`, cash green `#01D651`, loss
red `#FF4F5E`. Buttons are rounded rectangles (16 px) with a thin lit top
edge and a shaded foot (`src/components/gloss.tsx`), 52 px tall; the quiet
button is a dark fill with a hairline rim.

Type: OmenUI (the interface face), Inter for figures (its plain zero reads
better), and Inter in the agent chat. Scale: titles 25 / 32, headings 18,
body 15, secondary 13.

Logo: the transparent textured white O/dot mark. Voice: concise, direct.
Find signals. Trade assets. Put dividends in motion.
